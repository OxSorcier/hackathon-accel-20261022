Descriptor: **A headless agent for physical operations.**

## Product promise

An agent that knows your business, works through your tools and uses market intelligence to coordinate a response and execute the actions you approve.

Works through your existing tools.

Headless means the agent's company knowledge, reasoning, tools, approvals and durable actions are independent of a single user interface. The web workspace is one interface for review and demonstration. Specific third-party tool connections require their own integration and verification; this vision does not establish universal connector availability.

## Who it is for

Teams that buy, make, move and sell physical goods.

First: raw materials and construction supply chains, including timber, steel and building materials.

## Why now

Critical information arrives too late. Missing materials stall production, deliveries and projects.

The product is intended to bring market intelligence into decisions early enough for industrial teams to protect commitments, coordinate a response and qualify opportunities. This states the received problem and offer, not a measured customer result.

## Customer value

Keep production and projects moving.

Protect commitments. Capture new demand.

Help operational teams anticipate change and make strategic decisions.

## First-user acquisition proposal

Targeted outreach to operations and procurement leaders. Start with one critical supply workflow, then expand across sites and teams.

This is a proposed route, not acquired customers or confirmed pilots. The earlier three-pilot count was not a competition requirement.
