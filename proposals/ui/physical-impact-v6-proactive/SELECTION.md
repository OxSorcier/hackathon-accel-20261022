# Physical Impact: selected proactive pitch

22 October 2026. Version 6. Direct user go-ahead after the Why now refinement, with proactive asynchronous preparation made explicit.

## Main explanation

An agent that knows your business and uses market intelligence to prepare a coordinated response before you ask. It executes the actions you approve.

## Proactive market-intelligence card

Tracks market changes in the background to identify risks and opportunities.

## Why now

Market information is abundant. Agents can now connect it with company data and business tools, helping teams act before shortages stop production, deliveries or projects.

## Preserved selection

The selected A composition, Physical Impact name, tagline, customer-value line, WHO IT IS FOR, FIRST USERS and existing-tools note remain. The first-user route is direct outreach to operations and procurement leaders at French multi-site materials businesses, a first supply-planning pilot and expansion across their sites. Accel remains the receiving jury, not an acquisition partner.

## Received product meaning

The agent works in the background to gather relevant changes and prepare a company-specific briefing and coordinated plan before the user requests it. The user can inspect sources and alternatives, then approve actions. Approval, execution, verification and retained knowledge remain distinct parts of the full vision. Async preparation and headless execution do not replace that vision.

## Proof scope

The existing narrow verified-flow line is unchanged. This marketing-copy refinement does not create a new proof of automatic reports, proactive preparation, a connected ERP action or customer outcomes. Runtime verification stays with the current application owners and their receipts.
