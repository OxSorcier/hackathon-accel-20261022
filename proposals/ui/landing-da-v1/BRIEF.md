# AdaptOps landing page visual direction

22 October 2026. Version 1. Three reference-led raster proposals.

## Received direction

White space, deep navy typography, petrol action buttons, mint accents and a visible prepared company brief. The user selected the visual family of pitch-slide-v7-company A and B. The current deliverable extends that received direction into landing page proposals.

All customer-facing copy is English. The page sells the product offer: understand company context, follow market changes, assess risks and opportunities, coordinate a common plan, execute approved actions, verify results and retain decisions.

- A-airy-split.png: split hero with promise and prepared response, outcome strip, three-step approach and audience/CTA band. Recommended landing page base.

## Shared visual rules

- Background: white and pale mint.
- Typography: Inter-like sans serif, navy headings, generous spacing.
- Colors: navy #061B2B, petrol #075564, mint #63E5BD, pale mint #EFFAF6.
- Product: thin borders, restrained shadows, meaningful English labels, an explicit human approval step.
- CTA: Discuss a pilot. Secondary: Explore the approach.
- Outcomes: protect customer commitments, capture new demand, keep teams aligned.
