# Physical Impact: clean slide explorations

22 October 2026. Three English raster proposals based on the user-supplied clean off-white, evergreen and mint reference.

## Received goal

Sell the full company-aware market-to-action vision clearly and confidently. Headless execution remains complementary. Keep the three jury questions visible: audience, why now and acquisition route. No internal implementation warning or diagnostic banner belongs on these visual proposals.

## Shared promise

Physical Impact. Adapt as fast as your market changes.

An agent that knows your business, turns market intelligence into a coordinated response and executes the actions you approve.

Works in your workspace and through your existing tools.

## Product pillars

1. Knows your business: your stock, suppliers, customers and commitments.
2. Sees what matters: market changes, risks and opportunities.
3. Coordinates the response: one plan across procurement, operations, sales and finance.
4. Acts with approval: execute in your tools, verify results and retain decisions.

## Jury footer

Who it is for: Teams that buy, make, move and sell physical goods. First: timber, steel and construction materials.

Why now: Late information can stop production, deliveries and projects.

First users: Targeted industry outreach. Start with one supply workflow, then expand across sites.

The first-user route is proposed acquisition copy, not acquired users.
