## Shared offer

An agent that knows your business, uses market intelligence to coordinate a response and executes the actions you approve.

Keep production and projects moving. Protect commitments. Capture new demand.

Works in your workspace and through your existing tools.

## Four pillars

1. Knows your business. Links stock, suppliers, customers and commitments to your business priorities.
2. Sees what matters. Connects sector news, supply changes and new demand to your risks and opportunities.
3. Coordinates the response. Compares options across procurement, operations, sales and finance while protecting existing commitments.
4. Acts with approval. Executes approved actions in your tools, checks the result and retains decisions.

## Jury answers

**Who it is for:** Teams that buy, produce, transport and sell physical goods. Starting with raw materials and construction supply chains: timber, steel and building materials.

**Why now:** Market information is abundant, yet industrial teams often receive it too late to act. Missing materials can stop production, deliveries and construction projects.

**First users:** Direct outreach to industrial operations and procurement leaders. Start with one supply-planning pilot, then expand across sites and teams.

The acquisition text is a proposed route rather than acquired customers.
