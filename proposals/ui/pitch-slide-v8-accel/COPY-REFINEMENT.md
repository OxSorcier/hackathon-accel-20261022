# AdaptOps: Accel slide copy refinement

22 October 2026. English copy proposal. Updates the received slide and landing positioning; no image has been regenerated for this refinement.

## Received market scope

**Who it is for**

Teams managing inventory, supply and customer commitments.

**First market**

Raw materials and construction supply chains: timber, steel and building materials.

The product audience is broad. Operations and procurement are a concrete initial entry point, with sales, logistics and finance involved in shared decisions.

## Why now

**Market information is abundant. Turning it into timely business action is still a manual task.**

AdaptOps brings market intelligence into the company's decisions and workflows, helping teams act while the information can still make a difference.

This is proposed marketing wording for Erwan's received problem statement, not a measured claim about every business.

## User value

**Help operations teams anticipate change and make strategic decisions.**

The agent knows the business, works across authorised connected tools and uses market intelligence to coordinate a response and execute the actions the user approves.

## How to reach the first users

**Reach operations and procurement leaders through targeted industry outreach. Start with one recurring stock or supply decision, then expand across their sites.**

This is a proposed acquisition plan. The form asks how the team would reach its first users. It does not require three design partners. The earlier three-partner count and founder-introduction route were suggestions, not an Accel requirement or evidence of acquired users. Specific existing industry introductions can strengthen the plan once confirmed.
