## 2. The problem

**The market changes. Your teams need a common response.**

A supplier incident, a new project or a shift in demand can affect purchasing, stock, delivery promises and sales at the same time. The useful question is what your business should do next, with the resources and commitments it already has.

## 3. How it works

**Start with the company.** Connect the authorised facts that define your operations: sites, products, suppliers, customers, stock, projects, commitments and rules.

**Follow the changes that matter.** Bring relevant industry news, regional projects, tenders and internal changes into a company briefing, with sources and dates.

**Understand the impact.** See which commitments are exposed, which opportunities need qualification and what the available evidence supports.

**Choose one coordinated plan.** Compare actions across purchasing, operations and sales. Check quantities, dates and costs while protecting existing commitments.

**Approve, act and reassess.** Review the plan, approve actions within your authority and follow their recorded results. Revisit affected work when the facts change.
