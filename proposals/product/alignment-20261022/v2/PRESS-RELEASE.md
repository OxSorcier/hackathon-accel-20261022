## 1. Headline

AdaptOps turns market changes into coordinated operational plans

## 2. Standfirst

Adapt as fast as your market changes. AdaptOps connects industry signals with a company's operations, helping teams protect commitments, assess opportunities and execute approved actions.

## 3. Release

AdaptOps is developing an agent for businesses that need to respond when their market changes. It brings relevant external information together with the company's products, sites, suppliers, customers, stock and commercial commitments, so teams can move from a signal to a practical response.

A supply disruption can threaten one customer while creating demand elsewhere. A new project can be commercially attractive but require stock already promised to another order. AdaptOps is designed to assess these interactions in one plan, with quantities, dates, costs and the commitments that must remain protected.

The first focus is timber and building-materials businesses operating across multiple sites in France and Europe. Operations and purchasing lead the response, working with sales, logistics and finance on the decisions that concern them.

The experience starts with a prepared company briefing: what changed, why it matters, what remains uncertain and which actions are ready for review. Users can inspect sources, correct an assumption and compare options. Approved actions are bound to current data and permissions, with a recorded result and reassessment when conditions change.

The hackathon demonstration is being assembled around a synthetic six-site company and a controlled industrial incident. Its target journey combines protection of current orders with a qualified commercial request, then an explicitly simulated action and durable readback. Synthetic facts, public sources and simulated effects are identified separately.

## 4. Company description

We help businesses adapt as fast as their markets change, turning live signals into coordinated plans and executing approved actions.
