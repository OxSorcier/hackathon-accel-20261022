## UX/UI requirements for filming

Direction A is retained. Pitch-slide variants do not approve a new application design. Keep one UI owner and an integrator-owned candidate. New report/plan flow proposals must be received before their broader implementation.

1. **Prepared work first.** Compact company context, what changed and the next decision before the secondary question box. Counts are read from the actual company. Keep data origin/freshness visible and technical IDs in details.
2. **Business reading order.** "What changed", "Why it matters", "Recommended plan". Sources, calculations and traces use progressive disclosure.
3. **Honest impact labels.** Risk, opportunity and mixed effect distinguish verified facts, hypotheses and missing information. Do not fabricate an opportunity or action result for a screenshot.
4. **One plan across teams.** Actions show quantities, cost, timing, dependencies, constraints, authority and the next available step.
5. **Separate decision and effect.** Prepared, approved, executing, executed, failed, uncertain and superseded have distinct meanings where supported. A direct model run is not an executed business action. The current "Direct execution" run-mode badge is a targeted UI correction.
6. **Verify the result.** Confirmation names the effect and scope; success shows a returned receipt and reread outcome. Repeat clicks do not duplicate the effect. Unknown outcomes stay visible with an appropriate next step.
7. **Readable English and navigation.** Legible density, keyboard focus, consistent copy and narrow-layout behaviour. Aside is the first browser for snapshots, clicks, screenshots and DOM/CSS checks.

The [UI checkpoint](../../ui/ADAPTOPS-DEMO-CHECKPOINT.json) contains real 3001 evidence and the three priorities. Before filming, receive required independent code reviews and integrated browser checks. Branding alone proves none of the missing backend capabilities.
