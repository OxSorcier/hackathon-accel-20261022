## 2. The problem

**The market changes. Your teams need a common response.**

A supplier incident, a regional project or a shift in demand can affect purchasing, stock, delivery promises and sales at the same time. Your next decision depends on the company you already run: its resources, commitments and rules.

## 3. How it works

**It starts with your business.** Physical Impact is designed to retain authorised knowledge of your sites, products, suppliers, customers, projects, stock, commitments and policies. The next signal arrives in a company context that is already known.

**Follow the changes that matter.** Relevant industry news, regional projects, tenders, capacity announcements and internal updates enter a dated, sourced briefing.

**Understand risk and opportunity together.** See which commitments may be exposed, which opportunities need qualification and which links remain uncertain. A positive headline does not automatically create an opportunity for your business.

**Review one coordinated response.** Compare purchasing, allocation, transport and commercial actions. Check available resources, quantities, dates, spending and contribution while preserving existing commitments.

**Approve, act and adapt.** Review the plan and authorise eligible actions. The target product executes through authorised tools, records returned results and revisits affected work when the facts change.

## 4. First industry

**Built around the realities of timber and materials.**

Our first focus is multi-site businesses in France with European supply relationships. Operations and purchasing need the same facts as sales, logistics and finance, from an incoming purchase to the customer promise it supports.
