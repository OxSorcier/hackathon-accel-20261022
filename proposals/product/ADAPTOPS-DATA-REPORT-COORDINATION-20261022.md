## Data tranche

Reuse the prepared 6-site, 24-programme, 10,000-order-line portfolio. Enrich the dependencies needed for one coherent scenario, not every record indiscriminately.

- ERP: purchases and inbound dates/statuses, reservations, compatible products and exact units, independent alternatives, attributable quotes with quantities, prices, dates and validity.
- Company dossiers: projects, sites, owners, commitments, policy and stable record/source identities. Only released current facts enter the product.
- Industry relationships: supplier, industrial establishment, product and purchase dependencies. A supplier with another name may still share the affected establishment; independence must be established or unknown.
- CRM: a fictional affected competitor, relevant accounts and one complete qualified incremental request, with product, quantity, need date, responsible person, selling price and cost inputs. Other leads may remain unqualified.
- Provenance: source system, native ID, company scope, revision, observation/effective date, synthetic or public origin, verification status and source references.

The portfolio owner sends a proposed field structure and a small current sample before the final v2 manifest. The integrator receives the minimal intake contract from that exchange. This avoids a circular wait between a finished dataset and a contract needed to produce it.

## Scenario and evidence boundaries

A supplier-side industrial incident also affects a neighbouring competitor in the declared demonstration scenario. AdaptOps assesses existing commitments and separately qualified demand, then prepares a joint supply and commercial response.

Public incident, private commercial relationship, production interruption, inbound ETA and customer demand are distinct facts. Each link needs its own attributed evidence or explicit synthetic scenario status. The future-capacity ROCKWOOL publication does not establish a current delay. A negative competitor event alone establishes no customer order.

Operator-only future events and expected answers stay out of the imported current snapshot and model context. Controlled events are released through the authorised operator path. A source consulted now retains its actual publication and event dates; a recorded historical scenario is labelled as recorded.
