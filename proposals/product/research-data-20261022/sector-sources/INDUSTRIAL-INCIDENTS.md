## Conditions for the AdaptOps scenario

Keep the public observation separate from internal facts. An actual supplier relationship, damaged lot, delivery delay or nearby competitor needs its own evidence. In the explicitly fictional demonstration, those relationships may instead be attributed scenario data and visibly labelled. A negative industrial event does not establish additional demand.

For a calculated risk, receive the exact product, unit, purchase line, quantities, dates, current confirmation and all commitments sharing the resource. For a positive branch, receive the CRM need, quantity, requested date, compatible usable capacity and commercial terms. Protect existing commitments in the same calculation. Silo capacity is not a measure of stock lost.

The integrator receives source, product and time-horizon compatibility before ingestion. Data owns accepted public intake; the existing portfolio owner owns the attributed synthetic enrichment. The product/brief owner keeps the AdaptOps narrative and scenario label. No competing portfolio, source registry or scheduler is created here.

The fictional fire can be an operator-controlled event, with real public sources shown as separate observations. A historical source may support a clearly dated recorded scenario; its later known outcome must not be presented as a contemporaneous prediction. No artificial current supplier delay is attached to a publication about a future plant.
